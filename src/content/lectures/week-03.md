## Outline

- affordance, signifier, convention, and invention, defined against a
  doorknob and a drop shadow rather than in the abstract
- two failures: something that looks pressable and isn't, and something
  pressable that doesn't look it
- what the diagnosis asks: find one real ambiguous element and name which
  convention it broke
